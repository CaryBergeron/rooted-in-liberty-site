What we collect

When you request a free book, we collect your first name and email address. We do not store your IP address for this.

How we use it

We use your email to send the book you requested, news about new books in the Rooted In Liberty Library, and occasional offers. We use visit information to understand which pages are helpful and to measure our advertising.

What we never do

We never sell or rent your personal information.

Your choices

Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. To ask us to delete your information, reply to any of our emails.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to send email and host this website. They only use your information to provide those services to us.
